Read Aloud

The live list of chats, mute switches, and the on/off control open in a separate window. This sidebar cannot receive those updates.

Turn the plugin off and on once so that window opens. It is at http://127.0.0.1:47321.

Working means the model is still in the turn, including while it thinks. Speech starts after the turn finishes, unless that chat is muted, Read Aloud is off, or the microphone is on.
